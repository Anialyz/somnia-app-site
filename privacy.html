隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

Somnia — 冥想助眠与白噪音

生效日期 / Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. 概述 / Overview

Somnia 是一款用于放松、冥想、专注和睡眠的环境音频应用。你无需向我们提交姓名、邮箱或联系人等个人资料。 下文说明 App、Apple 和 Google 在提供本服务时如何处理相关数据。

Somnia is an ambient-audio app for relaxation, meditation, focus, and sleep. You do not need to provide us with personal details such as your name, email address, or contacts. The sections below explain how the app, Apple, and Google handle data while providing the service.

2. App 数据与保留 / App Data and Retention

播放偏好、混音预设、计时器和本机试用及订阅状态保存在你的设备上。App 不要求注册账户，也不读取联系人、麦克风或精确位置。卸载或清除 App 数据可移除本地数据。

Playback preferences, mixes, timers, and local trial and subscription status are stored on your device. No account is required. Somnia does not access contacts, the microphone, or precise location. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes local app data.

为提供配置更新、运行分析及故障排查，App 可向 VITANIA 的服务发送随机生成的安装标识、平台信息、会话开始事件及崩溃堆栈。网络服务和音频下载服务也会接收处理请求所需的 IP 地址及网络请求信息。我们不把混音预设或计时器设置作为这些事件上传。引导语音可由 Microsoft Azure Speech 合成；目前发送的是 App 预设的引导文本，不是麦克风录音。

For configuration updates, operational analytics and troubleshooting, the app may send a randomly generated installation identifier, platform information, session-start events and crash stack traces to VITANIA services. Network and audio-delivery providers receive IP addresses and request information needed to serve requests. Mix presets and timer settings are not uploaded as part of these events. Guided speech may be synthesized by Microsoft Azure Speech using predefined app guidance text, not microphone recordings.

本地数据由用户控制；服务器运行及诊断数据仅用于上述服务目的。关于适用的保留安排、访问或删除请求，请通过下方邮箱联系我们；我们可能需要你提供足够信息来定位相关记录。Apple、Google 及网络服务提供方依各自政策处理其服务数据。

You control local app data. Server operational and diagnostic data is used for the service purposes above. Contact us below about applicable retention arrangements, access or deletion; we may need sufficient information to locate relevant records. Apple, Google and network service providers process their service data under their own policies.

3. 订阅与支付 / Subscriptions and Payments

购买 App 内订阅时，iOS 交易由 Apple App Store 处理，Android 交易由 Google Play 处理。App 使用商店返回的购买状态提供权益，并在本机保存相应状态。VITANIA 不接收你的支付卡信息。订阅管理、续费和取消通过对应的 Apple 或 Google 账户进行；本机试用不自动产生订阅扣款。

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. The app uses store purchase results to provide access and stores the corresponding status locally. VITANIA does not receive your payment card details. Manage renewals and cancellation through the relevant Apple or Google account. The local introductory trial does not automatically create a paid subscription.

4. 广告与同意 / Advertising and Consent

Somnia 使用 Google Mobile Ads，仅在环境音频会话完全结束后，向符合条件的免费用户展示有限的插页式广告；广告绝不会中断正在播放的音频。 订阅用户和处于 Somnia 试用期的用户不会加载或看到广告。App 会在适用地区使用 Google 的同意流程，也不会仅为了展示情境式广告而请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限。Google 可能根据其政策处理设备信息、诊断信息、从网络数据推断的粗略位置以及广告互动数据。 Somnia 在此版本中不提供激励广告功能。

Somnia uses Google Mobile Ads to show limited interstitial advertising only to eligible free users after an ambient-audio session has fully ended. Ads never interrupt active audio. Subscribers and users in the Somnia trial do not load or see ads. The app uses Google's consent flow where required and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission merely to show contextual ads. Google may process device information, diagnostics, coarse location derived from network data, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies. Somnia does not expose a rewarded-ad feature in this release. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
